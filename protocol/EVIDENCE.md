# Evidence and compatibility limits

Wire identities and layouts were recovered from the matching May 10, 2013 RoF2 executable using static analysis and targeted client observations. Client executable SHA-256: `2a8702ad9f722704f01355c0750be7d6f164a8b9c9128ba0cf286ea32b405b0e`. No client code, executable, captures or personal account data is distributed.

Content is an adaptation of modern Live observations collected October 24, 2026. Selection previews cover 34 standard branches and 476 level variants. Active observations cover only the eight enabled first branches at all fourteen levels. Missing model identity, budgets or free ownership leave the other 364 variants disabled; shared class does not authorize copying an unevidenced branch loadout.

The content SQL retains evidence references as provenance labels. They refer to private research artifacts, not downloadable files supplied by this package. Captured native group/rank/string IDs, local adapter IDs and linked RoF2 spell IDs are separate concepts. Free highest-rank grants can use a local single-rank ownership adapter with a captured display rank; this is not proof of every lower native rank definition.

Paid-chain research captured exact costs, eligibility and effects. Unsupported Attack Speed, Enduring Voice and Improved Plane Shift are withheld rather than estimated. RoF2 spell differences remain explicitly documented in migration comments, including Warlord's Tenacity and selected free offensive spells; ordinary spell definitions are preserved. Historical or modern Live balance equivalence is not claimed.

Native acceptance is summarized in [STATUS.md](../docs/STATUS.md). Builds, SQL checks and UI inspection are distinct from combat, death, resurrection, group, mercenary and native-transform acceptance.
